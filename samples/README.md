# Sample documents

Original, public-domain sample PDFs for trying out Lumen. They were written for this
project, so they're free to use and redistribute.

| File | Topic | Pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `water-cycle.pdf` | The water cycle | 1 |
| `photosynthesis.pdf` | Photosynthesis | 1 |
| `neural-networks.pdf` | Neural networks & backpropagation | 1 |
| `french-revolution.pdf` | The French Revolution | 1 |
| `cell-biology.pdf` | Introduction to cell biology | 2 |
| `money-and-inflation.pdf` | Money, banking, and inflation | 2 |

You can also drop in any text-based PDF of your own — a textbook chapter, lecture
notes, a paper. Scanned PDFs (images of pages) won't work, since there's no OCR;
Lumen will detect that and tell you.
